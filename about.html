Meet who?

He Who Bridges Vision with Design.

My journey started with a Master’s in Visual Experiential Design, where I fell in love with branding, bringing ideas to life through identity, storytelling, and systems. Over time, I blended that with UX and business analysis studies in Canada to design with both users and strategy in mind.

At DigiBenders, I focus on building brand identities and design systems that are clear, memorable, and built to scale. Outside of work, I’m into skiing, sci-fi, and exploring AI tools for design.
